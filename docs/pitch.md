# GreenOps pitch and demo

**0:00–0:45 · The question**

We received 28 exports for one company. An avoided CHF and an avoided kilogram of CO₂e are different outcomes. GreenOps shows where they come from and tests whether a cost and carbon package survives operational constraints.

**0:45–1:45 · Establish the baseline**

Open the official challenge dataset. Show annual CHF and the location-based uncertainty range. Group drivers by tool and region. Open Data quality to point out missing costs, ledger provenance and AI invoice comparisons. Explain that the market-based view uses residual factors for covered regions and separately reports the Swiss gap.

**1:45–2:45 · Explain a decision**

Open Example questions. Show inactive AI seats in August and the run-rate calculation. Then show on-demand GPU operation and its link to development-cluster training. In Measures, locate the blocked idle `dr-failover` server. Ticket T-1094 says emergency recovery must stay running. Open the production question for fixed jobs and month-end closing, and show the Dublin freeze in the protection register.

**2:45–4:15 · Compare packages**

In Measures, run the planning case, then the conditional draft. Explain the day and CHF caps, annual versus first-year cash savings, and why the target shortfall is explicit. Select owner-approved only to show where evidence must be entered. A recorded approval requires a current evidence reference and verified safety checks.

**4:15–5:00 · Show a bonus and close**

Ask “top 5 tools by carbon” and follow with “and cost?”. Download the official CSVs. State the exact remaining target gaps from the latest challenge report and end with the next validation step shown by the review queue.
