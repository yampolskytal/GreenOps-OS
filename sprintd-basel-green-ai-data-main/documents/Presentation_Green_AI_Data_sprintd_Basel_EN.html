Expected outcome · Core use case

From exports to decision.

An IT manager loads her SME's exports. Your prototype does four things:

1 · Connect

Cloud, servers, AI and SaaS in one overall picture by department and tool.

2 · Find

The biggest drivers and the waste, backed up by the data.

3 · Decide

A package of measures with effort and savings in CHF and CO₂ (location-based), against target and budget.

4 · Know the limits

Respect protection windows, flag contradictions, hand over to a human when in doubt.

Bonus

Assistant, forecast, market-based view and clustering. Nice to have, not required.

Every figure is an estimate with an open calculation.
